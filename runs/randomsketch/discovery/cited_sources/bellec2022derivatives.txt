    \begin{equation}
    \E\Bigl[\Bigl(\brho^\top\bG\bfeta - \sum_{ij} \frac{\partial (\rho_i\eta_j)}{g_{ij}}\Bigr)^2\Bigr]

3. BOUNDED ONE-VECTOR SECOND-ORDER INEQUALITY
Published location: proof of Proposition 16, Appendix C, internal page 25, equation (C.6).
TeX location: colt_2022.tex lines 2756–2762, label eq:cor71.
Additional assumption: ||rho|| <= 1. Gaussian matrix and local Lipschitz assumptions remain as above.
Verbatim mathematical source block:

    \begin{equation}
        \E\Big| p \|\brho\|^2 - \sum_{j=1}^p
        \Bigl(\brho^\top\bG\be_j -\sum_{i=1}^n \frac{\partial \rho_i }{\partial g_{ij}}\Bigr)^2\Big|
        \le \C \sqrt p \Bigl( 1+ \E\sum_{ij} \Big\|\frac{\partial \brho}{\partial g_{ij}}\Big\|^2\Bigr)^{1/2}
        +\C \E\sum_{ij} \Big\|\frac{\partial \brho}{\partial g_{ij}}\Big\|^2.
        \label{eq:cor71}
    \end{equation}


Manuscript mapping: main.tex lines 2352–2371, label eq:one-vector-second-order, with rho -> u and p -> d. The source has sqrt(1 + E energy); the manuscript uses the weaker bound 1 + sqrt(E energy), which follows from sqrt(1+x) <= 1+sqrt(x) for x >= 0. The source uses an absolute constant, as does the manuscript. The transposed application exchanges n and d. No invalid strengthening is introduced by this mapping.

NORMALIZATION DISCREPANCY: DO NOT USE THE PRINTED PROPOSITION 14 AS A BLACK BOX
The published Proposition 14 (internal page 24), also in TeX lines 1812–1857, prints a SQUARED denominator (||h||^2 + ||psi||^2/n)^2 multiplying the raw derivative energy on its right-hand side. That expression is not scale invariant under a simultaneous nonzero rescaling of h and psi. The same source proof gives FIRST power in (C.5), as transcribed above. The manuscript explicitly cites the proof, states that it derives the scale-invariant form, and uses the first-order product divergence (C.3) rather than blindly copying the printed normalized statement. Evidence supports that distinction. The discrepancy is in the cited source's printed normalized statement; this evidence does not require changing the manuscript's claimed normalization.
